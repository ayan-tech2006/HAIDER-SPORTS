Home Policies Privacy Policy
Last Updated: October 2026 Your Privacy & Data Security

1. Introduction

At Haider Sports, we value the trust you place in us when shopping for cricket equipment. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, and utilize your personal information. Because we operate a 100% Guest Checkout system, we never require you to register a permanent user account.

2. What Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect only the necessary details required to fulfill your order:

  • Customer Name: To address your parcel and verification calls.
  • Contact Phone & WhatsApp: For order confirmation, delivery rider contact, and SMS tracking.
  • Delivery Address & City: To ensure accurate doorstep delivery by courier personnel.
  • Email Address (Optional): For dispatch receipts and order updates.
Zero Spam Guarantee: We never sell, rent, or trade your phone number or email address with third-party marketing agencies. Your contact details are used strictly for order logistics and customer support.

3. Third-Party Courier Sharing

To deliver your cricket gear anywhere in Pakistan, your shipping details (Name, Address, and Phone Number) are securely shared with authorized national courier services (TCS, Leopards Courier, M&P, and PostEx). These couriers are bound by strict delivery confidentiality agreements.

4. Local Browser Storage (Cookies & LocalStorage)

We use your browser's localStorage solely to remember your shopping cart items, saved wishlist items, and optional form pre-fills across visits. You can clear this anytime by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Not you? Clear" on our checkout page.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to request the removal of your order history, please reach out via email to privacy@haidersports.pk or WhatsApp +92 300 1234567.
