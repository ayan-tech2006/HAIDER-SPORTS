Shipping & Delivery Policy

Home Policies Shipping Policy
Last Updated: October 2026 Cash on Delivery Nationwide

1. Nationwide Delivery Across Pakistan

Haider Sports proudly delivers to over 50+ cities, towns, and districts throughout Pakistan. From Karachi and Lahore to Peshawar, Quetta, and interior Sindh/Punjab, our trusted courier partners ensure your gear arrives safely at your doorstep.

2. Delivery Charges & Free Shipping

  • Free Delivery: All orders with a subtotal of Rs. 5,000 or more qualify for 100% Free Nationwide Delivery.
  • Flat Rate (Below Rs. 5,000):
    • Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi: Rs. 200
    • Sialkot Local: Rs. 150
    • Faisalabad, Multan, Gujranwala: Rs. 220–230
    • Peshawar, Quetta & Other Cities: Rs. 250–280

3. Estimated Delivery Timeframes

Orders are dispatched from our central dispatch facility in Sialkot, Punjab:

  • Major Metros (Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi, Islamabad): 2 to 3 business days.
  • Other Cities & Towns: 3 to 5 business days.
  • Customized / Knocked-In Bats: Additional 24–48 hours for machine oiling, 10,000 mallet knocks, and grip application.
Safe Cricket Gear Packaging: All cricket bats are packed in thick bubble cushioning, padded bat sleeves, and heavy-duty reinforced corrugated cartons to protect the edges and toe during transit.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD) Procedure

When placing a COD order, please ensure an authorized person with the exact cash amount is available at the provided delivery address. Courier riders accept cash payments only upon delivering the parcel.

5. Live Order Tracking

Once your order is verified and dispatched, you will receive your Courier Tracking ID via SMS/WhatsApp. You can also track your live shipment anytime on our Order Tracking Page.
