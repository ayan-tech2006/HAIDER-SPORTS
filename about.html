Proudly Crafted in Sialkot, Pakistan
Our Heritage & Craft

Forged for Power, Balanced for Champions

Founded in Sialkot — the undisputed global heartland of cricket manufacturing — Haider Sports was born out of a deep reverence for the game. We recognized that while Pakistani cricketers possess immense natural talent, finding authentic, master-graded cricket equipment at fair prices was often challenging.

Every Haider Sports cricket bat is sculpted from seasoned English and Kashmir willow, hand-pressed to ensure optimum ping and rebound, and shaped with generous 38mm–42mm edges while maintaining an feather-light pickup.

Whether you are stepping out for club cricket on a Sunday morning, playing tape ball under lights, or competing in regional first-class tournaments, Haider Sports gear gives you the confidence to command the crease.
